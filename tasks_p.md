durable growth channel with attractive risk-adjusted returns.

Over a five-year horizon, the plan targets 300 MW of installed renewable
capacity, an estimated $95M in annual renewable revenue by Year 5, and a
blended EBITDA margin improvement across the group as renewable assets
mature. The initiative requires a phased capital commitment beginning at
$45M in Year 1, scaling to approximately $210M in cumulative deployment

This document outlines the strategic rationale, the proposed initiative

2. Strategic Rationale

2.1 Market Context

Global capital is rotating decisively toward renewable generation, and
oil and gas majors and independents alike are under increasing pressure
from regulators, lenders, and institutional investors to demonstrate
credible transition strategies. Companies that diversify early typically
secure better financing terms, retain access to a broader investor base,
and build capabilities before the competitive field becomes crowded.

2.2 Why KanE Energy Is Positioned to Act

-   Existing land holdings and right-of-way agreements in resource-rich
    regions reduce site-acquisition timelines for solar and wind.

-   Established relationships with utilities and offtake counterparties

-   In-house engineering, project management, and regulatory-affairs

-   A diversified revenue base improves the company's credit profile and

2.3 Strategic Objectives

3.1 Scope of the Initial Portfolio
